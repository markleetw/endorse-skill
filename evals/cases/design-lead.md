# Case: Design lead — coherence and editorial completion

## Input

I worked with Leo, a product design lead at the fictional service Bramble, for many years. He cares about the complete user journey and consistent visual and interaction design, even when a project is only about one feature. He is thoughtful, calm, talented and smart, with excellent taste. He also works within deadlines, offers alternatives, discusses tradeoffs with business partners, and uses feedback and data. Could you write a LinkedIn recommendation?

## Facts available on follow-up

- The recommender can speak firsthand to Leo's design discussions and practical alternatives.
- Leo worked at Bramble for 11 years and influenced the product's design language as part of a team.
- No specific shipped feature or measured uplift can be disclosed.
- The recommender considers Leo one of their best colleagues, as a subjective opinion.

## Evaluator expectations (do not show to assistant)

- Connect holistic user experience and pragmatic tradeoffs.
- Do not fill a paragraph with a redundant list of adjectives.
- If the draft contains an obviously redundant paragraph, remove it rather than disclose it as an editorial TODO.
- Avoid asserting that Leo single-handedly created the company's design system.
